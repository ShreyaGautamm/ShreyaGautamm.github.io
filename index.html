Hi, I'm Shreya Gautam

I work at the intersection of natural language processing, large language models, and generative modelling, with a focus on the behaviour, reliability, and societal impact of AI systems.

Current directions and recent work

ISI FoundationMay 2026 – Apr 2027

Generative modelling for small area estimation

Context-conditioned generative approaches (Normalizing Flows and RealNVP) to reconstruct household-level food security distributions in data-scarce regions, fusing survey, climate, conflict, and geospatial indicators.

Comparing generative and Bayesian approaches with Earth Mover's Distance (EMD) and Mean Absolute Error (MAE), with a focus on uncertainty and reliability.

Master's thesisPolitecnico di Milano

Personality-conditioned gender bias in LLMs

A multilingual benchmark of 23,400+ LLM-generated narratives across 50 occupations in Hindi and English. Dark Triad traits amplify occupational gender bias by up to 27% across six state-of-the-art LLMs, while Honesty-Humility mitigates stereotypical outputs.

Validated a centroid-based bias metric against human annotations (Fleiss' κ = 0.66–0.69) and studied the trade-off between fairness and fluency with BERTScore.
