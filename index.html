Hi, I'm Shreya Gautam

I work at the intersection of natural language processing, large language models, and generative modelling, with a focus on the behaviour, reliability, and societal impact of AI systems.

I'm a Research Scientist at ISI Foundation in Turin, where I work on generative modelling for humanitarian applications. I completed my MSc in Computer Science and Engineering at Politecnico di Milano in March 2026, where my thesis explored gender bias in persona-conditioned LLM narratives across English and Hindi.

My research journey began with NLP, focusing on AI-generated text detection, deception detection, and model behaviour, with research internships at the University of South Carolina and Dalhousie University. My work has appeared at ACL and EMNLP, including a paper that received the EMNLP 2023 Outstanding Paper Award.

Publications

Peer-reviewed conference papers and preprints (2023–2027)

2027 EACL 2027 ARXIV

Personality Shapes Gender Bias in Persona-Conditioned LLM Narratives Across English and Hindi: An Empirical Investigation

Kumar, T.*, Gautam, S.*, Chadha, A., Jain, V., & Pierri, F. (* equal contribution)

A multilingual benchmark of 23,400+ LLM-generated narratives across 50 occupations in English and Hindi. Finds that Dark Triad traits amplify occupational gender bias by up to 27% across six state-of-the-art LLMs, while Honesty-Humility mitigates stereotypical outputs, evaluated via a novel centroid-based metric and BERTScore.

arXiv:2604.23600 ↗
2025 ACL 2025 STUDENT RESEARCH WORKSHOP

SEPSIS: I Can Catch Your Lies – A New Paradigm for Deception Detection

Rani, A., Dalal, D., Gautam, S., Gupta, P., Jain, V., Chadha, A., Sheth, A., & Das, A.

Introduces SEPSIS, a novel deception detection framework modeling linguistic leakage cues, psychological markers, and cognitive load indicators across multi-party conversational dialogue to differentiate deceptive claims from truthful narratives.

2023 EMNLP 2023 🏆 Outstanding Paper Award

Counter Turing Test (CT²): AI-Generated Text Detection is Not as Easy as You May Think – Introducing AI Detectability Index

Chakraborty, M., Tonmoy, S. M. T. I., Zaman, S. M. M., Sharma, K., Barman, N. R., Gupta, C., Gautam, S., Kumar, T., Jain, V., Chadha, A., Sheth, A. P., & Das, A.

Examines the vulnerability of state-of-the-art AI-generated text detectors against evolving LLMs under adversarial perturbations. Introduces the AI Detectability Index (ADI) to establish a calibrated benchmark for measuring detector reliability and robustness.

2023 ACL 2023 LONG PAPERS

FACTIFY-5WQA: 5W Aspect-based Fact Verification through Question Answering

Rani, A., Tonmoy, S. M. T. I., Dalal, D., Gautam, S., Chakraborty, M., Chadha, A., Sheth, A., & Das, A.

Presents a structured aspect-based fact verification approach that decomposes multimodal news claims into 5W aspects (Who, What, When, Where, Why), leveraging targeted question-answering pipelines to provide fine-grained, explainable fact-checking verdicts.

Kumar, U., Kumar, T., Gautam, S., & Pandey, S. C.
