Generative Modelling for Small Area Estimation

Reconstructing household-level food security and socioeconomic indicators in data-scarce and conflict-affected regions. We apply context-conditioned generative approaches—specifically Normalizing Flows and RealNVP—to model complex multimodal distributions without unrealistic Gaussian assumptions.

The system fuses ground-truth household surveys with heterogeneous satellite imagery, climate indices, and conflict event databases. We benchmark generative models against classical Bayesian hierarchical estimators using Earth Mover's Distance (EMD) and Mean Absolute Error (MAE), focusing on calibration under distribution shift.

Personality-Conditioned Gender Bias in LLMs

An empirical benchmark evaluating how psychological persona conditioning influences occupational gender stereotyping in frontier LLMs across English and Hindi. We generated and benchmarked 23,400+ narratives across 50 standardized occupations.

Our findings demonstrate that persona prompts encoding Dark Triad traits (Machiavellianism, Narcissism, Psychopathy) amplify gender bias by up to 27% across six state-of-the-art models. Conversely, conditioning on Honesty-Humility mitigates stereotypical outputs by up to 18%. Validated with a centroid-based vector metric against human annotators (Fleiss' κ = 0.66–0.69).

Core Research Pillars

Three key questions guiding my scientific investigations.

1. Evaluation First

Standard benchmark numbers often mask catastrophic failure modes. My research designs stress tests, counterfactual perturbations, and centroid metrics that expose the true boundaries of model capability.

2. Societal & Humanitarian Focus

Applying machine learning where it matters most: climate resilience, food security distributions, and algorithmic fairness for marginalized and non-Western demographic contexts.

3. AI Safety & Detection

Investigating synthetic vs. human content boundaries, model hallucination vulnerabilities, and behavioral shifts when language models adopt adversarial personas.
