About Me

A bit of background on my academic path, research trajectory, and technical skills.

I am a Research Scientist at ISI Foundation in Turin, Italy, leading research in generative modelling for humanitarian applications and food security.

In March 2026, I completed my MSc in Computer Science Engineering at Politecnico di Milano, where my master's thesis investigated personality-conditioned occupational gender bias across 23,400+ LLM generations in English and Hindi.

Prior to that, I earned my BTech at Birla Institute of Technology, Mesra, and carried out research internships at the University of South Carolina and Dalhousie University. My paper on AI-generated synthetic text detection was awarded the Outstanding Paper Award at EMNLP 2023.

I am motivated by rigorous scientific evaluation: understanding what generative models truly learn, where they fail, and how to build reliable, fair systems for human wellbeing.
